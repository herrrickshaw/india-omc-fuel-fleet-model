# Valuing the petrol freed by ethanol blending as export revenue

Ethanol blended into petrol frees domestic petrol (MS) that India — a net product exporter with surplus refining — can sell abroad at FOB prices. Freed volume = ethanol blended = blend% × domestic petrol volume (54.05 bn L / 40 MMT). Valued at India's own petrol-export realisation (PPAC RR Table 4.11).

## 1. India's petrol-export trend (RR Table 4.11)

| FY | Exports (MMT) | Value (₹ cr) | Value ($ bn) | Realisation (₹/L) | ($/tonne) |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 2020-21 | 11.6 | 36,530 | 5.0 | 23.3 | 431 |
| 2021-22 | 13.5 | 81,649 | 10.9 | 44.8 | 807 |
| 2022-23 | 13.1 | 102,800 | 12.9 | 58.1 | 985 |
| 2023-24 | 13.5 | 92,957 | 11.2 | 51.0 | 830 |
| 2024-25 | 15.8 | 98,379 | 11.6 | 46.1 | 734 |

Petrol exports grew from 11.6 MMT to **15.8 MMT (₹98,379 cr)** over five years. Realisation swung ₹23–₹58/L with crude — central (FY24-25) **₹46.1/L (~$734/tonne, ~$86/bbl)**.

## 2. Petrol freed by blending, and its export value

| Blend | Freed petrol (bn L) | Freed (MMT) | vs current exports | Export value @ ₹46/L (central) | ($ bn) | range ₹23–58/L |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| E20 | 10.81 | 8.0 | +51% | ₹49,834 cr | $5.9 bn | ₹25,187–62,806 cr |
| E25 | 13.51 | 10.0 | +63% | ₹62,293 cr | $7.3 bn | ₹31,484–78,508 cr |
| E30 | 16.21 | 12.0 | +76% | ₹74,751 cr | $8.8 bn | ₹37,781–94,209 cr |

- At **E20**, blending frees **8.0 MMT** of petrol — **51% of India's current 15.8 MMT petrol exports** — worth **~₹49,834 cr ($5.9 bn)/yr** at FOB. In effect, ethanol has *already enabled roughly half of the petrol India exports today.*
- Pushing to **E30** frees **12.0 MMT** (~₹74,751 cr / $8.8 bn), which would roughly **double** exportable petrol vs the E20 level.

## 3. How this stacks against the other flows in this repo (E20, ₹ cr/yr)

| Flow | ₹ cr/yr | Beneficiary |
|---|--:|---|
| **Freed-petrol export value** | **~49,834** | OMC/refiner forex earnings |
| State VAT foregone (ethanol vs petrol) | ~17,900 | states (loss) |
| Central excise foregone | ~21,500 | centre (loss) |
| OMC extra pump throughput income | ~760 | OMC |
| State SGST-on-ethanol @5% (proposed) | ~3,200 | states (gain) |

The export value dwarfs the domestic tax flows — the freed petrol is the single largest rupee item ethanol blending creates, and it accrues as **export/forex earnings** rather than domestic tax.

## 4. Caveats

- **Export vs import-substitution:** the freed petrol can either be exported (valued here) *or* let India cut crude imports — the same barrels, valued at a similar FOB/import-parity price. This is the export lens as requested, not additive to an import-saving claim.
- India already blends ~E20, so today's 15.8 MMT export is *already net* of that; the E20 row is best read as 'ethanol has enabled ~8 MMT of current exports', E25/E30 as the incremental frontier (assumes refining runs flat and demand is displaced, not cut).
- Realisation is FOB petrol (~$86/bbl); it swings with crude — the ±band spans the FY20-25 range. Export excise (RR Table 8.6A, periodic) and freight are not netted.
- Freed volume = ethanol volume (blend%×domestic petrol), consistent with the sibling models; second-order mileage effects are ignored here.

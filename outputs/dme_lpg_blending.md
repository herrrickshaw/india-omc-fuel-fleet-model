# DME blending in LPG — the fourth quadrant of the Volume Dividend

DME (dimethyl ether) is now blendable into LPG up to 20% under **BIS IS 18698:2024**, with the engineering anchored by **LERC (LPG Equipment Research Centre, Bengaluru)**: material compatibility cleared at 20% DME (vapour phase), flex-fuel burner trials stable, and a measured **thermal-efficiency decrease of 5.26% at DME20** on the IS 4246 protocol — the cooking-gas analogue of SIAM's E20 mileage figure. DME carries **37% less energy per kg** than LPG (28.8 vs 45.8 MJ/kg).

## 1. Blend energetics and the cylinder

| Blend | MJ/kg | Energy vs LPG | LERC-basis usable drop | 14.2-kg cylinder energy | Extra kg for same cooking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DME5 | 44.95 | −1.86% | −1.31% | 638 MJ | +1.33% |
| DME10 | 44.1 | −3.71% | −2.63% | 626 MJ | +2.7% |
| DME20 | 42.4 | −7.42% | −5.26% | 602 MJ | +5.55% |

LERC's measured drop (−5.26% at DME20) runs *below* the pure energy math (−7.4%) — oxygenated DME burns cleaner and partly compensates, exactly as E20's octane recovery softens its energy penalty. We use the LERC basis as the central case.

## 2. Who pays: household and subsidy arithmetic (DME20)

- A 7-cylinder/yr household needs **+0.39 cylinders ≈ ₹312/yr** at an unchanged ₹803 cylinder price — the same invisible-levy mechanism as E20, but on a merit good.
- Nationally (whole 29.7 MMT pool blended): **+1.65 MMT extra LPG-equivalent bought = ₹9,325 cr/yr** of extra consumer spend.
- **The exchequer PAYS, not collects**: PMUY's ₹300/cylinder on 10.3 cr beneficiary households adds **≈₹1,201 cr/yr** of extra subsidy — the volume dividend inverts. Ethanol's dilution earns the exchequer excise; DME's dilution bills it.
- Honest pricing: a DME20 cylinder should sell at **₹761** (vs ₹803) for cooking-energy parity.

## 3. Does DME clear energy-parity economics?

- Energy-neutral DME price = LPG price × (28.8/45.8) = **₹32.7/kg** against LPG import parity ₹52/kg.
- Methanol-route DME (1.4 t methanol/t) costs **₹33.6/kg in feedstock alone** at imported methanol ₹24/kg — above the ₹32.7 parity ceiling before conversion, logistics or margin. **Imported-methanol DME cannot clear energy parity**; it merely swaps a propane import for a methanol import at a worse ₹/MJ.
- The route that works on paper: coal-gasification methanol (Coal India/BHEL projects, ₹15–18/kg) or bio-DME from syngas — domestic molecules, but carbon-heavy (coal) or immature (bio). Import substitution is the honest rationale ONLY on those routes.
- At DME20 nationally, DME demand is **6.27 MMT/yr** — ~6× India's current methanol production; the supply side does not exist yet.

## 4. Verdict in the Volume Dividend framework

| Blend | Dilution | Fuel's fiscal status | Volume effect lands on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethanol in petrol | −34%/L | heavily taxed | consumer pays, exchequer+trade collect |
| Biodiesel in diesel | −8%/L | taxed | mild, lands on freight |
| CBG in CNG | ~0%/kg | lightly taxed | nobody — the honest blend |
| **DME in LPG** | **−37%/kg** | **subsidised** | **consumer AND exchequer pay** |

DME-LPG is the worst quadrant: it combines ethanol's dilution problem with a subsidised fuel, so the hidden levy hits households (regressive — LPG is the Ujjwala merit good) *and* the subsidy bill simultaneously. If pursued for import substitution, two guardrails are non-negotiable: **(1) energy-parity cylinder pricing** (₹758 for a DME20 14.2-kg cylinder at today's ₹803) — or equivalently more kg per cylinder; **(2) domestic-carbon DME only** (coal/bio routes), since methanol-route DME just re-denominates the import bill.

## 5. Caveats

- LERC's 5.26% is a burner thermal-efficiency figure at DME20 (IS 4246); field cooking behaviour and older stoves may differ; we scale it linearly to DME5/10.
- IS 18698:2024 caps blends at 20%; higher shares need elastomer/valve changes (DME attacks conventional seals) — LERC compatibility work is the binding reference.
- Cylinder price and PMUY parameters are levers (Delhi ₹803, ₹300 subsidy); the whole-pool blend is a ceiling scenario — a phased urban rollout scales linearly.
- LPG LHV varies with butane:propane season mix (45.5–46.3 MJ/kg); DME 28.4–28.9.

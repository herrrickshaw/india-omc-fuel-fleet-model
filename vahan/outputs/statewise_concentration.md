# How concentrated is India's vehicle fleet across states?

Vahan4 CY2026 (YTD), 36 states/UTs; national ~18.1 million registrations. Cumulative (Pareto) share by ranked states, and an HHI concentration index per fuel (HHI 0–10,000; >2,500 = concentrated).

## 1. Overall fleet — cumulative share
| Ranked states | Cumulative share of all vehicles |
|---|--:|
| Top 3 | 33% |
| Top 5 | 47% |
| Top 10 | 71% |
| Top 15 | 87% |

- **Top 5 states = 47%** of all registrations; **top 10 = 71%**. It takes **6 states to reach half** the national fleet and **13 to reach 80%** — moderately concentrated, with a long tail of small states/UTs.

## 2. Concentration by fuel — top-5 share & HHI
| Fuel | Top-5 states | HHI | How concentrated |
|---|--:|--:|---|
| CNG | 64% | 1,056 | concentrated |
| StrongHybrid | 52% | 799 | moderate |
| EV | 49% | 715 | dispersed |
| Diesel | 47% | 666 | dispersed |
| Petrol | 47% | 669 | dispersed |
| Total | 47% | 667 | dispersed |

- **CNG is the most concentrated** — a handful of CGD-network states (Maharashtra, Gujarat, UP, Rajasthan, Delhi/Haryana) hold the bulk; small states have almost none.
- **Strong hybrids and EVs are concentrated** in the big/affluent + e-rickshaw states.
- **Petrol and diesel are the most dispersed** — every state runs two-wheelers and CVs, so they track population/total more evenly.

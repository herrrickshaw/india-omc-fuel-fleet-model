## The read

- **Uttar Pradesh is the biggest market overall** and leads **petrol** and **EV** by count — its EV lead is e-rickshaws/e-2W, not e-cars.
- **Maharashtra leads diesel, CNG and strong-hybrid by count** — the premium & commercial fuels.
- **By EV *share*, the east/northeast lead** (Assam, Tripura ~17%) on e-rickshaws; Delhi ~12% is the top big-state on e-cars/2W.
- **CNG penetration is highest in Haryana, Gujarat and Delhi** — the mature CGD-network states.

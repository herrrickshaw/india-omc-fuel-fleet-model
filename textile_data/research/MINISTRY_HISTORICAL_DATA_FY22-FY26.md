## SECTION 5: VALIDATION OF CAPEX-TO-EXPORT LAG

### **The 2-Year Execution Gap (Evident from Historical Data)**

**Capex Announcement Timeline → Export Realization**:

| Timeline | Event | Export Impact |
|----------|-------|----------------|
| **Sep 2021** | PLI scheme announced (₹10,683 Cr) | No impact FY22-23 |
| **FY21-FY24** | Government schemes + some private capex | Minimal export growth (+2.5% CAGR) |
| **FY24** | Global slowdown + competition from Vietnam | Exports DIP to $36B |
| **FY25** | Capex announcements ramp (PM Mitra, RIL, others) | Exports slowly recover to $37.5B |
| **FY26** | Capex wave in full force (₹44K Cr announced in FY25 realized) | Exports +2.1% only (still sluggish) |
| **FY27-28 (Projected)** | Production capacity comes online (BPCL LLDPE, Indorama, RIL O2C) | Exports expected +8-12% as capex translates to output |
| **FY30 (Target)** | Full ecosystem maturity | Exports target ₹50-60B possible (requires +8-15% CAGR FY26-30) |

**Critical Insight**: **Capex lags export impact by 2-3 years.** FY26 is the "investment spend" year (₹44K Cr+ realized), but exports not yet benefiting. FY27-28 will be the inflection when production ramps. This validates earlier analysis that margin recovery is FY28-FY30 event, not imminent.

## SECTION 9: SECTOR OUTLOOK — CORRECTED FOR HISTORICAL LEARNINGS

### **FY26-FY30 Revised Projection (vs. Earlier Bullish Forecast)**

**Earlier Projection**: +15-20% CAGR textile exports FY26-30
**Revised Projection**: +6-10% CAGR textile exports FY26-30

**Rationale**:
1. **Capex-to-Export Lag**: 2-3 year historical delay evident (FY25 capex → FY27-28 export benefit)
2. **Vietnam Competition**: Still strong (+21% FY26, bidding for #2 spot). Not losing share to India as fast as hoped.
3. **Bangladesh Disruption**: Slower materialization (political chaos ≠ immediate order shift). Some orders finding alternative sources, not just India.
4. **Commodity Pressure**: Cotton/yarn/fabric segment (40% of exports) flattening (+0.4% FY26) = structural margin compression
5. **Government Scheme Lag**: New TEEM/Tex Eco not disbursing until FY28+

**Corrected FY26-30 Trajectory**:
- **FY26**: $38.2 B (+2.1%) ✓ Actual
- **FY27**: $39.5-41 B (+3-7%) — Modest, trade agreements ramping up, capex still in construction
- **FY28**: $43-47 B (+8-15%) — Inflection, production coming online (BPCL LLDPE, RIL O2C, IOCL fiber)
- **FY29**: $48-54 B (+10-15%) — Full ecosystem benefit, margin recovery materializing
- **FY30**: $55-65 B (+12-18%) — Peak execution if all capex delivered

**3-Year Export CAGR (FY26-FY30)**: +13% (vs. ₹3.16 Lakh Cr base, reaching ~₹5-5.2 Lakh Cr by FY30, or $63-65 B)

## SECTION 10: KEY HISTORICAL LEARNINGS FOR DECISION-MAKING

### **1. Capex ≠ Immediate Export Growth**

**Evidence**: ₹44,019 Cr capex announced in FY25, yet FY26 exports grew only +2.1%. Historical pattern: 2-3 year lag before capex translates to export uplift.

**Action**: Patience required. FY27-28 are the inflection years, not FY26.

### **2. Commodity Segment (40% of exports) Faces Structural Headwinds**

**Evidence**: Cotton/yarn/fabric exports +0.4% FY26 (flat), 10-year commoditization trend. High capex focused on value-add (RMG, MMF, tech textiles) makes sense.

**Action**: Margin recovery depends on segment mix shift (commodity decline, value-add growth). Monitor RMG/MMF growth closely — they're the margin drivers.

### **3. Government Scheme Execution Lags Announcements by 2-3 Years**

**Evidence**: PLI announced Sep 2021, disbursements only ₹54 Cr by FY26. PCPIR target for 2025 missed by ₹6.6 Lakh Cr. TEEM/Tex Eco just announced, won't disburse meaningfully until FY28.

**Action**: Don't rely on new schemes (TEEM, Tex Eco) for FY26-27 support. Private sector capex (RIL, BPCL, mills) is the near-term driver.

### **4. Trade Agreements → Incremental Growth Only (+2-3 pp)**

**Evidence**: FY26 +2.1% growth, of which +2-3 pp came from trade agreements (UK +7.8%, UAE +22.3%). Suggests underlying demand flat. Capex needed to unlock next leg.

**Action**: Trade agreements are "nice to have," not transformational. Production capacity (capex) is the real catalyst.

### **5. Vietnam Momentum Continues Strong**

**Evidence**: Vietnam +21% FY26, bidding for #2 exporter status (vs. Bangladesh declining). India's +2.1% suggests market consolidation, not India market-share gains yet.

**Action**: Watch Vietnam closely. If Vietnam reaches $45B+ in FY26, India's target of $50-65B by FY30 becomes aggressive. Need FY28 capex payoff to stay competitive.

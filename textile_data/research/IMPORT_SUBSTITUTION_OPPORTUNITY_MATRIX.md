## SECTION 5: GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY FOR NEW ENTRANTS

### **Phase 1: FY26 (Contracting Phase)**

**Action Items for Companies Considering Entry**:

1. **Identify BPCL/Indorama/IOCL Supply Contracts** (Deadline: Jun 2026)
   - BPCL Bina LLDPE: Allocations opening for 3-5yr supply (first-come-first-served)
   - Indorama polyester: Long-term pricing now locked (early bird discount)
   - IOCL Bhadrak fiber: Dedicated supply channels opening
   - **Action**: Engage commodity procurement teams, finalize offtake agreements

2. **Secure Land & Infrastructure**
   - Target location near feedstock supplier (within 500 km BPCL, Indorama, IOCL)
   - Capacity: 10-25 acres for integrated facility
   - **Action**: Initiate land acquisition, environmental clearance process

3. **Design Capex Plan & Secure Financing**
   - Capex estimate: ₹300-800 Cr (depending on segment)
   - Financing options: Term loans (via banks), TEEM scheme support (₹5-10K Cr available), PM Mitra subsidies (40% machinery, 20-30% power, 60% water)
   - **Action**: Prepare detailed project reports, secure board approval

### **Phase 2: FY27-28 (Construction & Production Ramp)**

1. **Equipment Procurement & Installation**
   - Lead time: 12-18 months (FY26 orders delivered FY27-Q2)
   - Supplier coordination: European/Japanese equipment manufacturers
   - **Action**: Finalize equipment specs, place orders Q2 FY26

2. **Pilot Production & Quality Validation**
   - Months 1-6 of production (FY27-Q3/Q4): Limited volumes, quality testing
   - Buyer certification: Ensure key customers (apparel brands, downstream mills) validate product
   - **Action**: Engage quality/R&D teams for product optimization

3. **Ramp to Commercial Scale**
   - Months 7-12 of production (FY27-Q4 → FY28-Q1): Scale to 50-70% design capacity
   - Full capacity by FY28-Q3 (estimated)
   - **Action**: Recruit & train workforce, establish supply chains

### **Phase 3: FY28-30 (Market Capture & Margin Realization)**

1. **Secure Long-Term Customer Contracts**
   - Buyers: Downstream mills, apparel manufacturers, textile converters
   - Contract structure: 70-80% feedstock cost reduction passed to customer as ₹50-100/MT margin improvement
   - Volume: Target 60-80% product absorption before FY30
   - **Action**: Sales team engagements, pilot shipments to key buyers

2. **Optimize & Scale Further**
   - MARGIN REALIZATION: By FY30, achieve ₹150-300M annual profit per ₹500-800 Cr capex
   - Consider Phase 2 expansion (adjacent product lines, adjacent markets)
   - **Action**: Reinvest profits, plan next-phase capex

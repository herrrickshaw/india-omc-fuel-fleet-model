## Segment-by-Segment Margin Recovery Pathway

### **HOMETECH: ₹89% Margin → LOCKED ✓**

| Phase | Company | Status | Timeline | Margin | Risk Level |
|-------|---------|--------|----------|--------|------------|
| **Current** | Existing mills (Trident supplies 30-40% globally) | Spot-pricing | FY25 | ₹89% | Low (established market) |
| **FY26-27** | Trident land allotment + contract phase | Locked global buyers via Bharat Tex | FY26-27 | ₹89% | Very Low |
| **FY28-29** | Trident 50%+ capacity online + dyeing vats operational | Export order book ramping | FY28 | ₹89%+ | Low (first-mover advantage) |
| **FY30** | Trident 90%+ capacity + ecosystem ready | All volumes export-bound | FY30 | ₹89%+ | Very Low |

**Margin Protection Strategy**: HOMETECH needs NO feedstock substitution (already uses imported polyester + natural dyes). Trident's competitive edge = scale + integrated dyeing capacity (₹4,881 Cr investment). First-mover advantage: buyers lock suppliers in FY27-28 = 7-year cost premium for Trident.

---

### **CLOTHTECH: ₹15.8% → ₹66.7% Margin Recovery**

| Phase | Feedstock | Supplier | Timeline | Margin | Risk |
|-------|-----------|----------|----------|--------|------|
| **Current (FY25)** | 100% imported polyester chips + dyes | Saudi/China | Spot | ₹15.8% | Medium (tariff/supply risk) |
| **FY26-27** | Indorama polyester contracts locked | Indorama (60% supply) + imports (40%) | Long-term contract | ₹25-30% | Low (cost clarity) |

| **FY30** | Full domestic polyester ecosystem operational | Indorama 85% + RSVPM specialty 15% | ₹66.7% | Low (self-sufficient) |

**Critical Path**: Indorama's +0.8 MMTPA Paradeep + +0.4 MMTPA Kalyan expansions must complete on schedule (FY27-28). If 6-month delay = CLOTHTECH margin recovery pushed 1 year.

**Margin Recovery Driver**: Polyester chip cost drops from $1,200-1,300/MT (imported) → $950-1,050/MT (domestic + scale) by FY30 = $250-300/MT cost savings = 3-5% margin lift per segment.

- **Threat**: BPCL Bina (1.0 MMTPA) captures 50% market share by FY30
- **Price Response**: Likely drop $150-200/MT to compete with BPCL
- **Margin Compression**: 20-30% decline in volumes + pricing power
- **Timeline**: Crisis begins FY28 (BPCL first shipments)

**Import-Dependent Polyester Suppliers**
- **Current Position**: 40% of India's ₹8,000 Cr polyester market
- **Threat**: Indorama expansion + IOCL new fiber production capture 35% market
- **Price Response**: $50-100/MT competitive pressure
- **Margin Compression**: 10-15% decline
- **Timeline**: Gradual pressure (FY28 onward)

**Competing Textile Mills (Outside PM Mitra)**
- **Challenge**: Cannot match PM Mitra parks' cost infrastructure
- **Timeline**: Competitive pressure begins FY28, peak by FY30
- **Response Options**: 
  - Join Tier-2 parks (Virudhnagar, Warangal, Navasari) — smaller first-mover advantage
  - Invest in captive capacity (Indorama model) — high capex, 3-5yr to parity
  - Consolidation with PM Mitra park players — likely M&A path

## SECTION 2: FY26 TEXTILE EXPORT PERFORMANCE (LATEST DATA)

### A. Overall Textile & Apparel Exports

- **FY25 Total Exports**: ₹3,09,859.3 Cr
- **YoY Growth**: +2.1%
- **Absolute Growth**: +₹6,475.6 Cr

#### **Segment-wise Breakdown**:

| Segment | FY26 | FY25 | Growth % | Key Drivers |
|---------|------|------|----------|------------|
| **Ready-Made Garments (RMG)** | ₹1,39,349.6 Cr | ₹1,35,427.6 Cr | +2.9% | US tariff parity, BD disruption |

| **Tech Textiles** | ₹10,000+ Cr (est) | Similar | +5-10% (emerging) | Government GREAT scheme push |

### B. Market-wise Export Performance

**Strong Growth Markets** (FY26 vs FY25):

| Market | Growth % | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Egypt** | +38.3% | African textile demand boom |
| **Senegal** | +54.4% | Sub-Saharan Africa expansion |
| **Sudan** | +205.6% | Emerging market entry, ground floor |
| **Nigeria** | +21.4% | African market consolidation |
| **United Arab Emirates** | +22.3% | Re-export hub, Gulf demand |

| **Germany** | +9.9% | EU core market |

**Key Insight**: African + Asian markets are primary growth engines (emerging consumption, less competition from Vietnam/Bangladesh). EU markets growing modestly (post-trade agreement normalization).

### C. Trade Agreements Driving Export Growth

**New Bilateral/Regional Trade Agreements (FY26 execution)**:

1. **India–EFTA TEPA** (Oct 1, 2025)
   - Partners: Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein
   - Impact: Tariff reduction on textile machinery + inputs
   - Status: In force

2. **India–UK CETA** (Jul 2025)
   - Impact: UK textile imports preferential tariff (post-Brexit alignment)
   - Status: Signed (7.8% export growth to UK realized in FY26)

3. **India–Oman CEPA** (Dec 2025)
   - Impact: Gulf market access (UAE re-export hub benefits cascading)
   - Status: Signed (22.3% growth to UAE in FY26)

4. **India–New Zealand FTA** (Dec 22, 2025)
   - Impact: Emerging market access, Asia-Pacific corridor
   - Status: Announced (not yet in force; expected FY27 impact)

5. **India–EU FTA** (Jan 27, 2026)
   - **MAJOR**: Tariff parity with EU protocols, sustainability alignment
   - **Status**: Concluded (formal notification Feb 2026)
   - **Expected Impact**: +5-10% EU textile exports in FY27+ (once ratified by member states)

### D. Segment Winners & Laggards

**Winners**:
- **Ready-Made Garments (RMG)**: +2.9% (benefits from Bangladesh political disruption + US tariff parity)
- **Man-Made Textiles (MMF)**: +3.6% (PLI scheme driving new capacity, sustainability demand)

**Laggards**:
- **Cotton/Yarn/Fabrics**: +0.4% (commoditized, margin pressure, Indian cotton prices elevated)
- **Handloom**: Flat (government support sustaining, but global demand for handicrafts softening)

**Implication**: Structured segments (RMG, MMF) outperforming commodities → aligns with PM Mitra + PLI strategic focus on value-add.

## SECTION 3: UNION BUDGET 2026-27 NEW SCHEMES

### A. Integrated Programme for Textiles — Five-Part Strategy

**Status**: [Union Budget 2026-27: Strengthening India's Textile Value Chain](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2222927&reg=3&lang=1)

**Vision**: Strengthen entire value chain, target US$350B textile/apparel industry by 2030 (vs $160B current)

#### **Part 1: PM Mitra Parks**
- Already detailed (₹70,000 Cr, 7 parks)
- Budget 2026-27 allocation: ₹4,445 Cr (7-year outlay reaffirmed)

#### **Part 2: TEEM Scheme (Textile Expansion and Employment)**

**Objective**: Modernize traditional textile clusters, boost MSME participation

**Components**:
- **Machinery upgrade**: Capital support for weaving, processing, garmenting equipment
- **Technology transfer**: Adoption of modern techniques
- **Common testing/certification centers**: Quality compliance infrastructure
- **Cluster development**: Industrial parks for textile MSMEs

**Expected Outcomes**:
- Productivity gains: 20-30% per unit
- Quality improvement: BIS/international standard alignment
- Employment**: Large-scale MSME job creation (estimated 5-10 lakh jobs)

**Target Segments**: Traditional weaving (cotton, handloom), processing clusters, garment manufacturing hubs

**Budget Allocation**: TBD (scheme details being finalized per post-budget consultation reports)

#### **Part 3: Tex Eco Initiative (NEW)**

**Objective**: Future-proof India's textile exports against EU's environmental mandates + access green premium markets

**Drivers**:
- **EU Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation**: Ban on unsold textile destruction effective July 2026
- **Global ESG Demand**: Brands requiring carbon-neutral, recycled-content textiles
- **Price Premium**: Sustainable textiles command 5-10% price premium

**Components**:
- Sustainable fiber production (recycled polyester, bio-based fibers)
- Low-water dyeing technology
- Circular economy infrastructure (take-back, recycling)
- Carbon footprint measurement standards
- Green certification support (GOTS, OEKO-TEX, BLUESIGN)

**Expected Impact**:
- EU textile market capture: +5-10% (via sustainability compliance)
- Premium pricing: +5-10% margin on certified products
- Global market share: India repositioned as ESG-compliant supplier (vs. China/Vietnam image)

**Strategic Importance**: RE&UP ₹4,800 Cr Bharat Tex 2026 commitment is a signal that textile recycling ecosystem is being built (aligns with Tex Eco Initiative).

#### **Part 4: Export Facilitation & Trade Agreements**
- Already covered (India–EU FTA Jan 2026, others)

#### **Part 5: Skill Development & Labor Support**
- Budget allocation: TBD
- Focus: Textile technician training, garmenting upskilling

### B. Ministry Post-Budget Consultation (Feb 2026)

**Status**: [Ministry Holds Post-Budget Consultation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetails.aspx?NoteId=157185&ModuleId=3&reg=3&lang=1)

- **Participants**: 200+ industry stakeholders (mills, PSUs, state governments, unions)

## SECTION 10: KEY RECOMMENDATIONS BASED ON LATEST DATA

### **For Processors (Margin Recovery Strategy)**

1. **Lock BPCL Bina LLDPE Supply NOW** (Critical before environmental clearance completion)
   - FY26 export slowdown (+2.1%) → margin protection urgent
   - INDUTECH segment bleeding (-80.9% margin current) cannot wait for FY28
   - Timeline: Contracts finalize by Mar 2026 (before TEEM/Tex Eco funds divert capital)

2. **Pursue TEEM Scheme Support** (Machinery upgrade capital support, TBD allocation)
   - Expected: 20-30% productivity gains
   - Timing: Guidelines expected by Jun 2026, applications expected Jul 2026
   - Action: Prepare machinery/upgrade RFP now (ready to submit Jul 2026)

3. **Begin Tex Eco Certification** (EU sustainability for +5-10% margin premium)
   - Timeline: Programs roll out Jul-Sep 2026
   - Action: Start carbon footprint measurement + BLUESIGN certification applications
   - Payoff: EU-certified products command 5-10% price premium (validated by Indorama 2024)

### **For Investors (Updated Due Diligence)**

1. **Verify Export Slowdown Cause** (FY26 +2.1% below expectations)
   - Is it demand softness (recession risk) or supply/execution lag?
   - Company-level checks: Have announced capex come online on schedule?
   - Risk: If demand is soft, margin recovery by FY30 may compress 30-40%

2. **Monitor Bharat Tex 2026 MoU-to-Capex Conversion** (₹14,300 Cr committed)
   - Historical: 50-70% of MoU investment proposals convert to actual capex
   - Timeline: Expect ₹7-10,000 Cr actual execution by FY30
   - Watch: State government land allotment + clearances (make-or-break for timely execution)

3. **Prioritize Companies with TEEM + Tex Eco Eligibility** (New subsidy/support programs)
   - Companies in textile clusters (Tiruppur, Jaipur, etc.) = TEEM beneficiaries
   - Companies with sustainability roadmaps = Tex Eco beneficiaries
   - Double-subsidy upside: Government capex support + market premium

### **For Government (Execution Priorities)**

1. **Accelerate TEEM + Tex Eco Guideline Release** (Expected Q1 FY27, needs to be on time)
   - Delay by 2+ months = FY26 capex stalling (processors waiting for clarity)
   - On-time release = ₹5-10,000 Cr additional capex trigger

2. **Expedite India-EU FTA Ratification** (Concluded Jan 27, 2026; now in member-state ratification)
   - Each month delay = ₹200-300 Cr annual export growth opportunity lost
   - Fast-track through diplomatic channels (already concluded, just needs formalities)

3. **Monitor PM Mitra Infrastructure Completion** (₹564.72 Cr spent, on track)
   - Current pace: ₹564 Cr in ~6 months = ₹1,130 Cr/year

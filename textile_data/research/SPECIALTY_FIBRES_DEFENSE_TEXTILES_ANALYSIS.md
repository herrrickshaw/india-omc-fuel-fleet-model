## SECTION 2: DEFENSE TEXTILES APPLICATIONS & USE CASES

### Application 1: Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) - Ballistic Protection

**End-Use:** Body armor, helmets, protective vests for military/paramilitary
**Annual Demand (India):** 50,000-100,000 units/year
**Cost per Unit:** ₹5,000-15,000 (armor protection + aramid)
**Market Size:** ₹250-1,500 Cr annually

**Current Supply Chain:**
- Imported aramid (Kevlar, Twaron) from DuPont/Teijin: ₹100-120 Cr/year
- Domestic fabric converters (Trident, Arvind ≤5%): ₹5-10 Cr/year
- Gap: 80-90% import dependency

**FY30 Target:**
- Vibrant Polymers Phase 2: 500-1,000 KTPA aramid fiber capacity
- DRDO/HAL in-house textile units: 100-200 KTPA
- Startups: 50-100 KTPA
- **Total Domestic Supply by FY30:** 700-1,300 KTPA (50-60% of demand)

**Margin Recovery Path:**
- Current: Fabric converters 15-20% margin (import-dependent)
- FY30: Fabric converters 40-50% margin (domestic aramid + cost advantage)
- **Annual Profit Swing:** ₹80-120M for mid-scale converter (₹100-200 Cr capex)

**Risk:** Domestic aramid/carbon fiber production falls short of quality targets
- Probability: Medium (material science is complex)
- Impact: High (export competitiveness + margin recovery delayed)

**Mitigation:**
- ✓ Joint ventures with Teijin (aramid), Toray (carbon) — knowledge transfer
- ✓ Academic partnerships (IIT + AIIMS) for R&D validation
- ✓ Government R&D grants (DSIR, DPIIT) for de-risking
- ✓ Phased rollout: Pilot → commercial → export (3-phase validation)

**Contingency:** If domestic technology lags >2 years, shift to:

- Margin: 25-35% (lower than 40-55% vertical integration, but profitable)

- Probability: Medium-High (first-time capex, technology equipment sourcing)

### Risk 4: Price Competitiveness vs Imports (MEDIUM RISK)

- Probability: Medium (feedstock cost parity requires RIL/BPCL online)
- Impact: Medium (margin compression, extended ROI timeline)

**Mitigation:**

- ✓ Scale focus: Target 1,500+ KTPA by FY30 (sufficient for cost parity)

# Naphtha → BTX routes — the reformate-vs-petrol pivot, made literal

The petrol→petrochemicals substitution in these models isn't just an accounting swap of naphtha —
it runs through a **specific, shared unit: the catalytic reformer.** The same reformate stream either
raises petrol octane (blended into the fuel pool) *or* is extracted for BTX aromatics (benzene,
toluene, xylenes) that feed the chemicals India imports. As ethanol/EV cap fuel demand, that reformate
can be diverted to BTX — the mechanism, not a metaphor.

*Source: F. da Silva, "Naphtha & BTX (Benzene, Toluene, Xylenes): Refining Routes" (LinkedIn), plus
process-licensor references (Axens, UOP).*

## The production routes

| Route | Unit / technology | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Catalytic reforming** (straight-run naphtha, Pt/alumina, 500-550 °C) | CCR Platforming™ (UOP) · Aromizing™ (Axens) | **reformate** — high-octane *and* aromatics-rich |
| **Steam-cracking co-product** | pyrolysis gasoline (**pygas**) | supplementary aromatics feed |
| **Aromatics extraction** | extractive distillation → solvent recovery → fractionation | segregated **benzene / toluene / xylenes** |
| **PX maximisation** | **PAREX™** (adsorptive p-xylene separation) + **ISOMAR™** (xylene isomerisation) | **para-xylene** → PTA → PET |
| **Toluene upgrading** | **TATORAY™** (toluene transalkylation) | toluene → **benzene + xylenes** (upgrade low-value toluene) |
| **Integrated complex** | ParamaX™ (Axens) | benzene + PX with benzene-hydrogenation option |

## Why this matters — routes map straight onto India's biggest import gaps (DCPC FY24-25)

| Reformate/BTX route | Feeds → product | India import (DCPC, ₹ cr) |
|---|---|--:|
| **Xylenes → PAREX/ISOMAR → paraxylene** | PX → **PTA → PET / polyester** | PTA 13,501 + PX 7,965 + PFY 3,072 = **24,538** |
| **Benzene → ethylbenzene** | → **styrene** | **11,105** |
| **Benzene → cumene → phenol → BPA** | → **polycarbonate / epoxy** | PC 5,545 + epoxy |
| **Benzene → cyclohexane** | → caprolactam → **nylon** | (nylon/fibre) |
| **Toluene (imported ₹4,669) → TATORAY** | → benzene + xylenes (self-supply) | toluene 4,669 |
| **Benzene → nitrobenzene → aniline** | → **MDI (polyurethane)** | aniline 2,591 |

The single biggest substitutable cluster — **the polyester chain (PX→PTA→PET, ~₹24,500 cr)** — is
exactly what **PAREX + ISOMAR** on reformate xylenes produces. India already imports toluene (₹4,669 cr)
*and* the benzene/xylene derivatives; **TATORAY** would convert that surplus toluene into the scarcer,
higher-value benzene and xylenes — turning an import into feedstock.

## The economics (consistent with the earlier GRM lever)

- The article puts BTX/aromatics integration at **+$0.68–2.02/bbl** of crude — in the same band as the
  Digital Refining figure used here (+$1.5–2/bbl marginal GRM from petrochemical integration).
- **Global BTX market ≈ $12.7 bn by 2034, ~4.8% CAGR** — durable demand, higher-margin than gasoline.
- The reformer is already built and running for octane; **the marginal capex is the aromatics complex
  (extraction + PAREX/ISOMAR/TATORAY), not a new cracker** — a lower-cost path to the aromatics half of
  the substitution prize than a greenfield naphtha steam-cracker (which targets the olefin half: PE/PP/PVC).

## The through-line, tightened
- **Olefins half** (ethylene/propylene → PE, PP, PVC, MEG): from a **naphtha steam-cracker**.
- **Aromatics half** (benzene/toluene/xylenes → styrene, PTA/PET, polycarbonate, MDI): from the
  **catalytic reformer + aromatics complex** — the unit that today makes petrol octane.

Ethanol frees petrol → the reformate that raised its octane is freed too → routed through PAREX/ISOMAR/
TATORAY it becomes the paraxylene, benzene and xylenes India buys in dollars. **The petrol-to-chemicals
pivot has a specific address: the reformer.**

# India's forex on essentials — and the missing lever: a PLI for chemicals

India spent **$718 bn on imports (CY2024, DGCIS)**. Strip out gold and electronics and what remains is a bill of *essentials* the economy cannot switch off — energy, chemicals, edible oil, fertiliser — each a structural dollar drain with an import-substitution lever. This sizes that bill and argues the one big gap: **chemicals has no PLI.**

## 1. The forex bill — where the dollars go

| Import (CY2024) | US$ bn | % of imports | Essential? | Substitution lever | Under PLI? |
|---|--:|--:|:--:|---|:--:|
| Crude oil | 137 | 19% | ✅ | Ethanol/EV demand cap · domestic E&P (deepwater/Andaman) | no (ethanol EBP) |
| Electronics (HS-85) | 90 | 13% | — | PLI electronics/semiconductors — working | YES |
| Gold & precious stones | 60 | 8% | — | Discretionary — duty/financialisation, not substitutable | n/a |
| Chemicals (organic HS-29 + plastics HS-39) | 47 | 7% | ✅ | Crude-to-chemicals + freed ethanol feedstock | NO — the gap |
| Coal & coke | 32 | 4% | ✅ | Domestic coal · renewables · CBG | no |
| LNG + LPG (petroleum gases) | 30 | 4% | ✅ | Domestic gas · CBG (SATAT) | no |
| Edible / vegetable oils | 17 | 2% | ✅ | National Mission on Edible Oils / oilseeds | no (mission) |
| Fertilisers | 11 | 2% | ✅ | Domestic urea/nano-urea · Nutrient-based | no (subsidy) |

- **Essential-import forex ≈ $274 bn/yr** (~38% of all imports) — of which **energy is $199 bn** (crude + coal + gas) and **chemicals $47 bn**.
- Energy, edible oil and fertiliser each already have a substitution push (ethanol/EV, edible-oil mission, domestic fertiliser). **Chemicals — the 4th-largest essential drain and the fastest-growing — is the only one without a Production-Linked Incentive.**

## 2. The chemicals gap

- India's **14-sector PLI programme (₹197,291 cr / ~$23 bn)** covers electronics, pharma, autos, solar, textiles, food, telecom, steel and more — **chemicals & petrochemicals are not among them.**
- Yet India's chemical import bill is **₹631,898 cr (~$74 bn, MoC FY25)**, with **~₹125,000 cr (~$15 bn) of it petroleum-feedstock-substitutable** (PVC, PE, PP, styrene, polycarbonate, MDI/TDI, acrylonitrile, MEG — see the HSN-coded import tree).
- The template works: the **electronics PLI turned India from a mobile importer into a net exporter** in ~4 years. The same instrument, pointed at the chemical import list, is the obvious next move.

## 3. The case for a chemicals PLI (official intent)

- **FM Nirmala Sitharaman** has stated the government **"will consider a PLI scheme for chemicals and petrochemicals"** — the intent is on record.
- The **Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers is formulating** a scheme focused on **import substitution of key intermediates**, with a likely **4-6% incentive on incremental production** (per industry reporting).
- **PIB** records the government redrafting the Petroleum, Chemicals & Petrochemicals policy; **PCPIRs** (Petroleum, Chemicals & Petrochemicals Investment Regions) already provide the industrial-cluster backbone a PLI can plug into.
- **Specialty chemicals grow ~12% CAGR** — a PLI would capture that value at home instead of importing it.

## 4. Proposed design (grounded in this analysis)

| Lever | Proposal |
|---|---|
| **Target list** | The HSN-coded, high-import-dependence products: PVC `3904`, PE `3901`, PP `3902`, styrene `2902 50`, polycarbonate `3907 40`, MDI/TDI `2929 10`, acrylonitrile `2926 10`, MEG `2905 31`, epoxy `3907 30`, nylon `3908 10` |
| **Priority** | The ~85-100%-imported cluster first — polycarbonate, MDI/TDI, styrene, ACN — engineering plastics & polyurethanes (deepest gap, highest value) |
| **Incentive** | 4-6% on incremental production, tapering as capacity matures |
| **Feedstock link** | Tie to crude-to-chemicals / naphtha crackers and the ethanol-freed petrol pool — rupee feedstock displacing dollar polymers |
| **Cluster** | Anchor in PCPIRs; single-window + duty rationalisation alongside |

**The prize.** Substituting even half of the **~₹125,000 cr (~$15 bn)** petroleum-linked chemical imports saves **~$7-8 bn/yr of forex** — comparable to the entire ethanol programme's freed-petrol value, and permanent. A 4-6% PLI on that incremental output is a fraction of the forex it protects.

## 5. The through-line

India's essential-import forex bill is ~$274 bn, and the levers are being pulled everywhere except the chemical drain. **Ethanol frees petrol → the freed naphtha cracks into the very chemicals India imports → a PLI makes that domestic production bankable.** Rupee feedstock, rupee incentive, dollar imports substituted — the same rupee-vs-dollar logic as ethanol, applied one link downstream. Chemicals is the missing piece of a coherent import-substitution stack.
